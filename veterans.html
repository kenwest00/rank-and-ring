Who qualifies for the Veteran Program?

Any honorably discharged veteran of the U.S. Armed Forces who is:

Starting a new trade business (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing)
Currently operating a trade business and looking to grow
Transitioning from active duty into a trade career

Spouses and surviving spouses of veterans who operate a qualifying trade business are also eligible.

Military experience that often maps to trade careers: Air Force 3E1X1 (HVAC), Army 12P / 12N (Plumbing/Electrical), Navy CE / EM, Marine Corps 1141 (Electrician).
