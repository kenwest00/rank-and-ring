Phase 2

Onboarding — the first 14 days

Timeline
Days 1–14
Your program launches
We set up everything in 14 days. You review and approve.
  • Days 1–2: Onboarding call. We confirm your services, service areas, license number, insurance, and designated emergency contact
  • Days 3–5: Google Business Profile optimized. Unclaimed listings claimed, updated, and verified with your information
  • Days 5–10: Website built — 5 pages, mobile-first, license visible, service areas set, click-to-call on every page

Phase 3

The monthly rhythm — ongoing

Every month
Ongoing
While you do the work
Here's what happens every month without you having to think about it.
  • Week 1: Content posts go out (Nextdoor + social). You approved them the week before
  • Week 2: Review generation follow-ups sent to customers from the past 30 days via automated SMS
  • Week 3: Google ranking check, competitor snapshot, listing accuracy audit
  • Week 4: Monthly performance report delivered — calls, reviews, ranking, content reach, and next-month priorities
  • Growth/Pro: 30-minute (or 60-minute Pro) review call with Ken to walk through the report and plan next month
  • Anytime: emergency reputation issues get a 24-hour response (Pro plan)
