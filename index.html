Common Questions

Questions we hear before the first call.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days notice. We keep clients by delivering results, not by trapping them in contracts.

How long does it take to see results?

Most contractors see rating movement within 30–45 days and measurable search ranking changes within 60 days. Your website is live within 14 days of signing.

What if I already have a website?

We'll audit it in the free analysis. If it's solid, we optimize it. If it's hurting you, we'll tell you exactly why and what we'd do differently.

How does call triage know what's an emergency?

The AI is trained on trade-specific language — "AC won't turn on," "pipe burst," "no power." Those route to you immediately. Tune-ups and estimates book to your calendar.

Do I review content before it goes live?

Yes, always. You approve every post before it's published. We write it, you review it, we post it. Your voice, your standards.

Is there a veteran or graduate discount?

Yes. Veterans get 25% off + 2 months free with DD-214. Recent trade graduates get 20% off + 1 month free within 24 months of graduation.
